SplitShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

SplitShare helps you split trip expenses with friends and settle up in Indian rupees.

What the app stores

If you create an account, the app stores your name, mobile number and email, plus the trips, participants and expenses you enter. In this version all of this is stored on your own device, in the app's local storage. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. Guest mode keeps data only until you close the app.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Back up in your Profile, the app asks you to sign in with Google and saves a backup file of your trips in a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive. The app can access only this folder (the Drive "appdata" permission), not your other files. The developer cannot see your backup. You can restore it on another phone, and you can delete it from your Google Account settings under third-party apps and services. Your password is never included in the backup.

Contacts (optional)

If you tap Add from contacts, your phone's own contact picker opens. Only the names of the contacts you select are added to your trip on your device. The app cannot read your other contacts, and contact names are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or send your trip data to our servers or to third parties. The only place data leaves your device is your own Google Drive, and only if you choose to back up. Summaries are shared only when you choose to send them, for example through WhatsApp or email.

Your choices

You can delete your data by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app. Questions or requests: suneel.sre@gmail.com.
